Detailed Description

RHS for the mean curvature \(K\).

Combines advection, the covariant Laplacian of the lapse, and quadratic extrinsic curvature terms:

\[\partial_t K = \beta^i\partial_i K - \gamma^{ij}D_i D_j \alpha + \alpha(\tilde{A}_{ij}\tilde{A}^{ij} + \tfrac{1}{3}K^2). \]
