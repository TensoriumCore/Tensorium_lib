Detailed Description

Hamiltonian, momentum, and Gamma-constraint evaluation on the structured grid.

The constraints follow the vacuum ADM definitions expressed in terms of the conformal variables:

\[\begin{aligned} H &= R + K^2 - K_{ij}K^{ij},\\ M_i &= D_j K^j_{\ i} - D_i K,\\ C_i &= \tilde{\Gamma}^i + \partial_j\tilde{\gamma}^{ij}. \end{aligned} \]

compute_bssn_constraints first reconstructs physical-metric quantities ( \(\gamma_{ij}=\chi^{-1}\tilde{\gamma}_{ij}\), \(K_{ij}=\chi^{-1}(\tilde{A}_{ij}+\tfrac{1}{3}\gamma_{ij}K)\)) and then uses the derivative operators to evaluate the above expressions on every interior grid point.
