Detailed Description

template<typename K>
class tensorium_RG::BSSNAtildeTensor< K >

Computes the trace-free conformal extrinsic curvature tensor \( \tilde{A}_{ij} \) in the BSSN formalism.

This tensor is defined as:

\[ \tilde{A}_{ij} = \chi \left( K_{ij} - \frac{1}{3} \gamma_{ij} K \right) \]

where:

  • \( K_{ij} \) is the extrinsic curvature tensor
  • \( K = \gamma^{ij} K_{ij} \) is its trace
  • \( \chi \) is the conformal factor
  • \( \gamma_{ij} \) is the spatial 3-metric
